Built a production-shaped AI incident runbook generator as a portfolio project.

It turns alert context, metrics, recent changes, and logs into an evidence-backed runbook
with severity, probable cause, release-gate status, escalation steps, and Markdown/JSON
outputs. The repo includes a shared Python engine, CLI, FastAPI service, Prometheus
metrics, tests, Docker, Kubernetes manifests, Terraform scaffolding, and a CI template.
